# Night Signal · Seasonal idle
Open preview.html in a browser. No external dependencies or live integrations. Start screensaver to fade all 14 displays into a shared seasonal scene, clipped to their real key boundaries. Select a season to preview snow, pink spring petals, beach tides, orange autumn leaves, or rain. Pause motion freezes the scene. Escape/click wakes the device. Optional 10-second idle is a demo setting.

Design: 1.9-second content fade, 2.2-second scene fade. Steady low-contrast particles; no flashing, sound, or camera data. A single miniature seasonal character lives in the wide screen. Shared coordinates let particles travel naturally across displays while the chassis and gutters remain black. Beach water approaches from the bottom in slow layered sine waves, with subtle foam and sand visible above.

Production: suggested idle threshold 2–5 minutes, configurable. Native device button-down should wake without also executing its usual action; require a fresh press to activate. Honor reduce-motion and battery/CPU limits. Urgent alerts should interrupt the idle scene; defer ordinary notifications to a quiet indicator or configurable policy. These behaviors are design proposals, not connected in this demo. Pause rendering on device sleep/disconnect. The preview caps drawing resolution at device-pixel-ratio 2 and stops scene updates while hidden or awake. Reduced-motion users receive a static scene. Automatic season uses browser local calendar and Northern Hemisphere seasons; expose hemisphere and manual override in production. Rain is manually selectable, not driven by a live forecast.

Reuse the geometry/masking for the hardware's actual key resolution and topology. Preview text and widgets are illustrative. All artwork is editable/code-based; icons retain the Night Signal palette. Files may be used, modified and redistributed without attribution.
